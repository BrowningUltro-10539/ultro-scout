How outliers are flagged

Once a team has 4 or more matches, each match is compared with that team's median for every stat. A match is flagged when it is more than 3 elements away from the median and either far outside the team's usual spread (2.5 × MAD) or less than half / more than double the median. Flagged matches are named in Lookup and in the sheet. Clean avg leaves flagged values out. An average is marked skewed when it is far from the median. You can exclude a bad match completely (for example a scouting mistake) from Lookup or with the Exclude checkbox in the sheet.
